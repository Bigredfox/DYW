Scope · proof of concept · approved

Daily Work Log

A structured daily log so you can open a past week or day and see what you actually did—not reconstruct a semester from memory.

User, loop, and proof

Mikey logs his own CS project work. The demo lands when he can open a week and a weekday and read the six answers.

The kernel

Not a blank notes page. Six prompts, one after another. Structure makes it easier to write; a dedicated app is less boring than a Notion table you forget to open.

1. What you did 2. Skills learned 3. Obstacles 4. How you overcame them 5. Resources 6. People you spoke to

Who and why

Mikey, CS senior and team lead this semester. Long project, lots of tasks, review with a faculty coach: “I know I did it, but what did I do?” Today that’s memory (or notes that never get filled in). This experiment is his log, not a tool for reviewing teammates.

Now, later, cut

Open a row to see what’s in the proof of concept, what’s parked, and what was named as out.

Now — in the proof of concept
  • Friendly home screen and an inviting way into today’s log
  • Six guided prompts, one at a time, then save
  • Look back by week and by day in a simple list or table
  • Enough saved days to demo look-back — not a seeded full year
  • Local app is enough; deployment is optional
Later — worth doing, ranked
  1. AI weekly summary/report (vision also includes a daily recap, set-out vs. accomplished, improvement suggestions)
  2. In-app speech-to-text
  3. Weekly goals broken into daily goals, with an end-of-week check-in
  4. GitHub-style contribution heatmap

Laptop OS speech-to-text can be used during the PoC without building it.

Cut — named, with a reason
  • Reviewing teammates / manager dashboard — different product; he chose the self-log.
  • GitHub commits, issues, screenshots as evidence — kernel is structured answers, not an evidence locker.
  • AI skill tagging — skills are a field you fill in, not something the app infers.
  • Accounts, share links, internships as first audience — later product.
  • A full year of preloaded logs — vision is 52 weeks; the PoC only needs enough days to open a week and a day.
